Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about what Business for Lawyers does, how the Promise Ledger works, and what the year-long program actually involves.

Business for Lawyers provides practice management coaching, consulting, and systems advice specifically tailored for legal practice principals, partners, and practice managers in Australia. The service helps law firms fix broken operational processes, improve client intake, increase profit margins, and build sustainable law practices.

Law firms primarily lose clients and profit through broken promises — gaps in operational processes such as delayed intake, poor file updates, lack of follow-up, or inconsistent service. When internal systems break down, client trust erodes, leading to lost referrals, administrative bottlenecks, and reduced practice margins.

The Promise Ledger is a core principle at Business for Lawyers: trust doesn't average out. A single broken promise can sink a client relationship built on several kept promises. Firm growth depends on keeping every promise, not most of them, by embedding each one into a repeatable, reliable process.

It's a year-long program delivered in four stages. Weeks 1–4, Investigation: finding exactly where promises are being made, kept, or broken across client intake, matter delivery, financial visibility, and team accountability. Weeks 5–6, Planning: turning that into a committed plan for the year ahead. Weeks 7–12, Implementation: embedding that plan into how the firm actually runs. From Month 4, Quarterly Action & Review: tracking KPIs every quarter and building momentum, skills, and margin across the practice.

Roy leads Business for Lawyers. He has over twelve years of experience working exclusively with Australian law firm principals and practice managers to optimise firm operations, streamline delegation, and drive profitable practice growth.
